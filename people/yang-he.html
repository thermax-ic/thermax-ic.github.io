Biography

Yang He received the B.S. degree in optical information science and engineering from Shandong Normal University and the M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in physics from the Harbin Institute of Technology. After receiving the Ph.D. degree, Yang He worked at Yangtze Memory Technologies (YMTC). Yang He is currently a postdoctoral fellow / visiting scholar at Peking University.
