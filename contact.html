Collaboration & Measurement Requests

Please include a brief description of your sample, measurement objective, expected thermal property, sample structure, and available supporting information.

  • Sample material and layer structure
  • Approximate film thickness or device geometry
  • Target property: thermal conductivity, thermal boundary conductance, or thermal resistance
  • Available images, process information, or previous measurement data
  • Expected timeline and collaboration purpose
